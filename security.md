## 🛠 Feature Breakdown

### Feature 1 — Format Options
Choose from MP3, AAC, FLAC, or WAV depending on device and quality needs.

### Feature 2 — Quality Settings
Pick bitrate (128–320 kbps) and sample rate (44.1 kHz / 48 kHz).

### Feature 3 — Batch Conversion
Queue entire albums or playlists and convert in one pass.

### Feature 4 — Metadata Preservation
Keep title, artist, album, track number, and cover art.

### Feature 5 — Library Structure
Organize output as `Artist / Album / Track.mp3` automatically.

### Feature 6 — Cross-Device Use
Play offline on any MP3 player, phone, or DAW.
